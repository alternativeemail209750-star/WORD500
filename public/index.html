    How to play WORD500

    • The game picks a hidden word. Its length is shown by the row of blank tiles.
    • Everyone watching the TikTok LIVE types their guess directly in chat — a real word of the same length.
    • Every few seconds, the most-typed valid word becomes the audience's official guess for that turn.
    • A = right letter, right spot.
    • B = right letter, wrong spot.
    • C = letter isn't in the word.
    • The keyboard on screen colors itself in as clues come in, to help everyone plan their next guess.
    • The audience shares one pool of 15 attempts per word — guess it before time runs out!
